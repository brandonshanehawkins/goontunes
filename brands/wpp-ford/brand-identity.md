# Brand Identity Specification: WPP Ford Web Design System

This document defines the creative north star and branding guidelines for the **WPP Ford sub-brand**. These specifications are implemented programmatically in `wpp-ford-theme.css`.

---

## 1. Creative North Star: "Creative Precision"

The WPP Ford sub-brand represents a unique marriage between the sophisticated, gridded structure of WPP and the bold, active legacy of Ford. 
- **Creative and Bold**: We show up as a creative agency, not as our client. We are provocative and not corporate. We embrace negative space and dynamic imagery.
- **Unique and Ownable**: We connect WPP and Ford brands via typography and color, but we maintain a distinct personality in layout expression.
- **Modern and Relevant**: Contemporary, culturally connected, and visually striking.
- **Modular and Flexible**: Marrying structural layouts with energetic and clean styling.

Visual Style: Defined by a strictly flat depth, sharp rectangular divisions, bold outlines, and energetic solid color accent blocks behind type. Gradients are forbidden.

## 3. Typography Rules

Uses the custom **WPP** font-face (Black, Medium, Regular, Light, Thin).

- **Eyebrows & Labels**: All-caps, WPP Medium, wide letter-spacing (`0.2em` / tracking 200).
- **Short Headlines**: All-caps, WPP Black, tight leading (`0.85`), normal tracking (`0em`).
- **Long Headlines**: Sentence-case, WPP Black, tight leading (`0.85`).
- **Body Copy**: WPP Regular, clean, left-aligned, straightforward layout.
- **Accent Blocks**: Highlighting a single word or statement using solid background accent blocks behind the text to draw focus.

---

## 4. Shapes & Spacing

- **Border Radius**: Strictly `0px`. Rounding is forbidden.
- **Borders & Outlines**: Defined by thin, high-contrast solid borders (`1px solid var(--mcs-color-border)`).
- **Elevation**: Completely flat. Drop shadows are forbidden.
- **Grids**: Visual structure based on standard layout grids (Minimal, Small, Standard, Split configurations).
