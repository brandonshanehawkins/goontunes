# Brand Identity Specification: Lincoln Main Design System

This document defines the branding guidelines for the **Lincoln Main** client brand, used for luxury digital deliverables, vehicle showcases, and editorial interfaces.

---

## 1. Creative North Star: "Luxury Precision & Editorial Excellence"

The Lincoln brand represents quiet luxury, restraint, and extreme attention to detail.
- **Editorial Contrast**: High-contrast pairing of sophisticated editorial serifs with clean geometric sans-serifs.
- **Understated Premium**: Layouts emphasize grand imagery, generous breathing room (margins), thin elegant border dividers, and soft warm lighting.
- **Decisive Elegance**: Elements feature clean layouts, micro-radius shapes, and flat depth with luxury textures.

---

## 2. Core Color Palette

| Token Name | Hex Code | Visual Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lincoln Charcoal** | `#1A1A1A` | Core background surface or heavy body copy |
| **Lincoln Gold/Champagne**| `#D3C2A0` | Active borders, key hyperlinks, and primary highlights |
| **Warm White** | `#FBFBF9` | Clean canvas surfaces |
| **Muted Platinum** | `#ECEBE7` | Card panels and soft dividers |
| **Text Obsidian** | `#0D0D0D` | Headers and display typography |

---

## 3. Typography Rules

Uses **LincolnSerif** (Lincoln Miller) for editorial display and **LincolnFont** (Proxima Nova) for clean interface labeling.

- **Display & Headlines**: LincolnSerif (Lincoln Miller) Light or Roman, left-aligned or centered, showing classic serif elegance.
- **Eyebrows & Small Labels**: LincolnFont (Proxima Nova) Sbold or Regular, uppercase, with wider tracking (`0.15em`).
- **Body & Controls**: LincolnFont (Proxima Nova) Light or Regular.

---

## 4. Shapes & Structure

- **Border Radius**: Very slight rounding (e.g. `2px` on luxury buttons and inputs).
- **Outlines**: Super-thin borders (`1px solid var(--mcs-color-border)`) using gold or soft platinum colors.
- **Layouts**: High-end editorial showcases, vehicle gallery cards, and luxury brand assets.
