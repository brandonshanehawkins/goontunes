# Brand Identity Specification: WPP Main Design System

This document defines the creative playbook and branding guidelines for the parent **WPP Corporate Brand**.

---

## 1. Creative North Star: "Corporate Modernism"

The parent WPP brand style is defined by clean corporate modernism, structural simplicity, and restraint. Its storytelling is built around "The Dot" device—which represents creative transformation.
- **The Dot**: Used to punctuate bullet points, scale from micro to macro for creative expression, and frame gridded/symmetrical layouts.
- **Excess of Space**: Wimbledon/Cream and White should always be used in excess of Navy, giving layouts a breathing room.

---

---

## 3. Typography Rules

Uses the custom **WPP Sans** font-face (Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, Black).

- **Display Copy**: Thin weight, large format sizes only.
- **Titles**: Light weight, all-caps.
- **Headlines**: Light weight, sentence-case.
- **Body & Subheads**: Regular weight, sentence-case.
- **Footers**: Regular weight.

---

## 4. Layout & Spacing

- **Grid module**: 40px square grid with 40px margins and 40px column gutter spacing (tailored for 1920x1080 layouts).
- **Shapes**: Clean, slight border-radius is permitted.
- **Dot Expressions**: Gridded layout blocks can feature dot grids (symmetrical) or a bold micro/macro dot graphic.
