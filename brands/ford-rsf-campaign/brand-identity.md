# Brand Identity Specification: Ford RSF Campaign

This document defines the branding guidelines for the **Ford RSF Campaign** sub-brand.

---

## 1. Creative North Star: "Vibe-Based Durability"

The Ford RSF Campaign is a high-contrast, durable campaign style that is highly visual, "vibe-based", and ever-evolving. It leverages raw typography and strong color statements to create energetic advertising and youth-oriented messaging.

---

## 2. Core Color Palette

Inspired by the RSF campaign assets:
- **RSF Dark Grey/Black**: `#121212` (Primary structure and typography)
- **RSF Bright Orange**: `#FF5A00` (Campaign focus, highlight markers)
- **RSF Clean White**: `#FFFFFF` (Canvas backgrounds)
- **RSF Muted Slate**: `#5F6368` (Secondary information)

---

## 3. Typography Rules

Uses clean bold grotesque sans-serif elements for heavy impact:
- **Campaign Headlines**: Extra large display weights, high-contrast black labels.
- **Eyebrows & CTAs**: High-contrast outline borders with inline color fills.
