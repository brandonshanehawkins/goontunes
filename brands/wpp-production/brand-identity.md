# Brand Identity Specification: WPP Production Design System

This document defines the creative guidelines for the **WPP Production** brand.

---

## 1. Creative North Star: "Vibrant Ingenuity"

WPP Production brings content to life at scale. It visualizes an energetic, AI-powered future where ingenuity and creativity meet. 
- **Vibrant Creative**: We build vibrant worlds, real and imagined, driving innovation across everything we make.
- **Tonal Gradients**: We infuse our brand with an elegant, elevated energy using tonal mesh-gradients.
- **Layers & Scaling**: We use incrementally smaller layers to show progression, speed, and scale. We show gradients transition from a clear sphere to abstract organic forms.

## 3. Typography Rules

Uses the custom **WPP** font-face (Regular, Medium, Bold).

- **Hero Statements & Headlines**: WPP Medium (leading 1.1, tracking +15).
- **Intro Paragraphs**: WPP Medium (leading 1.1, tracking +15).
- **Subheads**: WPP Bold (leading 1.1, tracking 0).
- **Body & Legal Copy**: WPP Regular (leading 1.1, tracking 0).
- **Sentence Case**: All typography is set in sentence case. Lowercase everything except the first letter of a sentence, proper nouns, and product names.

---

## 4. Design Elements & Gradients

- **Mesh Gradients**: Created by blending Magenta, Pink, Orange, and Yellow on top of White/Grey backgrounds to create soft organic folds.
- **Depth & Layers**: Elevated, overlapping blocks combined with the dot storytelling device.
