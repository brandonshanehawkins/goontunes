2. The Bold Pitch

Jeffrey boasts to the patriarch that within one year, he will make the company perform better than ever and prepare it to go public.

The Patriarch

"If we do all that and go public, I'll make you Vice President."

Jeffrey

4. The Outcome & Legacy

  • Jeffrey delivers on his word—driving record performance, taking the company public, and earning the VP seat.
  • He keeps his promise and gives his newborn son the middle name Bobol (B-O-B-O-L).
  • Over time, "Bobol" transforms into a badge of family pride passed down through every direct lineage to honor ambition, achievement, and keeping one's word.
